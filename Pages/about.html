Our Story and Heritage

Founded in Komani(Queenstown), Ambesiwe Designs was created to honor traditional Xhosa fashion while bringing modern elegance to every garment we craft. from detailed beadwork to fitted attire, each piece tells a unique story of heritage, culture and quality craftsmanship.


Meet the founder and Master tailor
